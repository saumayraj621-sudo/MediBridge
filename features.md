# 🚀 MediBridge Features

MediBridge provides a unified healthcare coordination environment connecting patients, clinics, doctors, hospitals, and administrative teams through digital healthcare workflows.

---

## 1. Multi-Portal Healthcare System

MediBridge provides role-based portals designed for different healthcare stakeholders.

### Clinic Portal

The clinic portal allows healthcare staff to manage patients and initiate the referral process.

**Key capabilities:**

- Patient registration
- Patient information management
- Referral creation
- Referral status tracking
- Healthcare facility coordination

### Doctor Portal

The doctor portal provides healthcare professionals with access to relevant patient and referral information.

**Key capabilities:**

- View assigned referrals
- Review patient information
- Manage referral requests
- Update referral status
- Coordinate with healthcare facilities

### Admin Portal

The administrative interface provides centralized monitoring and management of the healthcare network.

**Key capabilities:**

- Monitor platform activity
- Manage healthcare users
- Monitor referrals
- Access platform analytics
- Manage healthcare facilities

---

## 2. Patient Registration & Management

MediBridge allows healthcare staff to digitally register and manage patient information.

The system helps maintain organized patient records and makes relevant information available throughout the referral workflow.

**Key capabilities:**

- Digital patient registration
- Patient profile management
- Patient information access
- Referral history
- Organized healthcare records

---

## 3. Digital Referral Management

The referral system is one of the core components of MediBridge.

Healthcare workers can create digital referrals and send patients to appropriate doctors or healthcare facilities.
