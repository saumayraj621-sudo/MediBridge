# Project Overview

## MediBridge

MediBridge is a unified digital healthcare referral and rural outreach platform designed to improve coordination between patients, clinics, doctors, and hospitals.

The project focuses on simplifying healthcare referral workflows and providing a centralized environment for managing patient information, referrals, healthcare facilities, and supporting services.

---

## Problem

Healthcare referrals between rural clinics, doctors, and hospitals can be difficult to manage when information is spread across disconnected systems.

Common challenges include:

- Difficulty tracking patient referrals
- Limited coordination between healthcare facilities
- Manual or fragmented referral processes
- Difficulty identifying appropriate healthcare facilities
- Limited visibility into referral progress
- Communication gaps between patients and healthcare providers

These challenges can make healthcare coordination slower and less transparent.

---

## Proposed Solution

MediBridge provides a centralized digital platform for connecting different healthcare stakeholders.

The platform brings together:

- Patients
- Rural clinics
- Doctors
- Hospitals
- Healthcare administrators

The goal is to provide a more organized digital workflow from the initial patient registration through referral and further care.
